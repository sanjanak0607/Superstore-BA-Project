# Superstore Sales Analytics Dashboard
### Excel | Pivot Tables | Interactive Dashboard | Business Insights

---

## Project Overview
This is an end-to-end business analytics project built on the Sample 
Superstore dataset from Kaggle. The goal was to go beyond just making 
charts — to actually analyze the data like a business analyst would, 
find what the numbers are saying, and turn those findings into 
actionable recommendations.

---

## Dataset
- **Source:** [Sample Superstore — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)
- **Size:** 9,994 orders | 21 columns | 2014–2017
- **Regions:** West, East, Central, South
- **Categories:** Technology, Office Supplies, Furniture

## Skills Demonstrated
- Data cleaning — TRIM, PROPER, date parsing, duplicate removal, 
  nested IF flags
- Advanced formulas — VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX MATCH, SUMIFS, 
  AVERAGEIF, LARGE, IFERROR, SUMPRODUCT
- Pivot Tables — nested rows, % of total, top 10 filter, 
  year × month matrix
- Interactive Dashboard — KPI cards, slicers, timeline slicer, 
  pivot charts
- Business Analysis — 5 key findings, 3 recommendations, 
  data limitations

---

## Key Findings
1. **West dominates** both revenue ($725K) and profit margin (14.94%) 
   — Central is the underperformer at 7.92% margin despite $501K in sales
2. **Furniture is bleeding** — $741K revenue but only 2.49% margin; 
   Tables and Bookcases are actively loss-making
3. **18.7% of all orders run at a loss** — likely driven by 
   aggressive blanket discounting
4. **Revenue grew 51%** from 2014 to 2017 with a consistent Q4 spike 
   every year
5. **Customer concentration is healthy** — top 10 customers = only 
   6.7% of revenue
